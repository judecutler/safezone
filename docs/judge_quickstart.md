# Try SafeZone in 60 seconds

Turn your sound on. The emergency part is spoken. A phone works best, and a narrow desktop browser window also works.

1. Open https://safezone-alpha-one.vercel.app/ and tap **Run the demo**. The demo asks for no permissions and uses recorded data, and every demo screen carries a DEMO banner.
2. Tap **Continue**. On each photo step, tap the shutter (it uses the recorded photo) or **Skip**. Then tap **Finish**.
3. On the review screen, read the room result. "What Muse Spark noticed" shows the model's reasoning. The safe-spot card shows that SafeZone's own checks set aside the model's floor spot because it was in a doorway. Tap **Save this place**.
4. On **My places** you see two demo places, Ocean Beach in San Francisco (recorded data) and Kamakura in Japan (sample data). Tap **Simulate an earthquake**.
5. Listen. The phone says "Drop, cover and hold on" with the room's spot, and nothing tells you to move. Tap **Shaking has stopped**.
6. Listen to the go-now line. Watch the demo walker follow the 10-minute route out of the hazard area with spoken turns. Tap **Walker speed 8x** to speed it up.
7. Tap **Shelters that fit your household**. These are labelled SAMPLE DATA, because FEMA listed no open shelters on the day we recorded.

To start again, tap **Leave emergency mode**, then **Exit demo** on the places screen.

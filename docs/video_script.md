# SafeZone demo video script (target 2:45, limit 3:00)

Before filming: run `python tools/record_fixtures.py` so every screen shows recorded real data, not SAMPLE data. Film on a phone with SafeZone added to the home screen. Every claim below is something the app does today; do not add any that it does not.

## Act 1. Prepare (0:00 to 1:25). Bright, calm UI.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Start screen, the cross-section drawing | "On the coast, an earthquake gives you two jobs. Survive the shaking. Then get out of the water's way. Almost nobody has worked out either one in advance." |
| 0:12 | Type the address, the building card appears | "SafeZone starts with your address. It checks California's official tsunami hazard map, San Francisco's soft-story list and OpenStreetMap, and links every flag to its source." |
| 0:28 | Tap young children, wheelchair, pets | "Four taps for who lives here. They change the plan later, so nobody answers questions in an emergency." |
| 0:36 | Photograph the room; the scanning screen | "Now one photo of each room. SAM 3.1 finds every object that matters in an earthquake. Muse Spark reads the room against a fixed safety rule set." |
| 0:50 | Overlay with red outlines and numbered hazards | "It found two tall wardrobes and a wire shelf that can tip, and boxes stored up high." |
| 0:58 | "What Muse Spark noticed" | "And it reasons about the room. The left bed is in the wardrobe's tipping zone. The cartons sit above the head of the right bed. The wardrobe by the door could block the way out." |
| 1:08 | Safe-spot card with the set-aside line | "Muse Spark suggested a spot on the floor. SafeZone's own checks set it aside, because that's where the wardrobe would land. The model proposes; code decides. Here there's no sturdy table, so the plan says what the official guidance says: interior wall, cover your head, and if you're in bed, stay there." |
| 1:20 | Tick "Move heavy items down low"; flash the fridge sheet | "Every fix comes from a fixed table, never from the model. Tick them off. Print the plan for the fridge." |

## Act 2. Emergency (1:25 to 2:25). Cut to the dark UI; a phone buzz marks the cut.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 1:25 | Tap the home-screen icon; DROP. COVER. HOLD ON. | "Now it's not a drill. During shaking, the app tells you one thing: Drop, Cover, Hold On, with your room's safe spot. Nothing on this screen tells you to move." |
| 1:38 | Tap "Shaking has stopped"; the red go-now text | "When the shaking stops: you're in a tsunami hazard area. Strong or long shaking means go now, on foot. Don't wait for an alert." |
| 1:48 | Switch on airplane mode; the route map with the live dot | "Networks fail. The plan doesn't. The route out of the hazard area is stored on the phone and drawn without map tiles, with your live GPS position." |
| 2:00 | Turn list and walking time | "SafeZone checks every route in code: it must end outside the official hazard area and must never lead deeper into it. The walking time uses a slow pace, for kids and older adults." |
| 2:10 | Shelter cards | "Then shelters that fit this household: wheelchair accessible only, none inside the hazard area, ranked by walking time and how full they are, each with its data timestamp." |

## Close (2:25 to 2:45)
